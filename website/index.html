04 A small field guide

Less setup.

Full controls & documentation
01

Start somewhere new.

Install the Windows alpha or unpack the portable ZIP. Launch Worldcraft and choose Create a new world or an offline AI example.

02

Step into your landscape.

Click the landscape to capture the mouse. Move with WASD, look with the mouse and press Tab to switch between walking and flight.

03

Make it your own.

Press E to edit, drag to paint, and use Ctrl + S to save. Esc releases the mouse. F1 opens help.
